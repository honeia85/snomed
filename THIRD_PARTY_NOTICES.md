# Third-party sources and rights

This repository contains author-generated code, figures and identifier-free derived metrics. It does
not redistribute the complete benchmark corpora, their mention text or their terminology-linked
annotations. The derived metrics were calculated from the following public sources and have been
modified by removal of mention text and terminology identifiers, system scoring and aggregation:

- **DisTEMIST corpus: detection and normalization of disease mentions in Spanish clinical cases.**
  Antonio Miranda-Escalada, Eulàlia Farré, Luis Gasco, Salvador Lima and Martin Krallinger.
  Concept DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.6408476. Version used here:
  https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7614764. Source record: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
- **MedProcNER/ProcTEMIST Corpus: Gold Standard annotations for Clinical Procedures Information
  Extraction.** Salvador Lima López, Eulàlia Farré Maduell, Luis Gascó Sánchez and Martin
  Krallinger. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7929830. [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
- **SympTEMIST Corpus: Gold Standard annotations for clinical symptoms, signs and findings
  information extraction.** Salvador Lima López, Luis Gascó Sánchez, Eulalia Farré, Laura Vigil
  Gimenez and Martin Krallinger. Concept DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.8223653. Version used
  here: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.8413866. [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
- **MedMentions.** Chan Zuckerberg Initiative. The source repository states that the data are
  released under CC0 1.0: https://github.com/chanzuckerberg/MedMentions.

Figure 1 contains a short manuscript-disclosed example derived from SympTEMIST; the English mention
wording is a study translation. It also contains terminology-description quotations solely to
explain the scoring example. Those quotations are not relicensed by this repository. SNOMED
CT-related rights and redistribution boundaries are described separately in `NOTICE_SNOMED.md`.
